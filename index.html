Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pure honey, ordering, and delivery.

How can I be sure your honey is 100% pure and raw?

Our honey is cold-extracted and never subjected to high heat pasteurization or ultra-filtration. It retains its natural microscopic bee pollen grains, live enzymes, and rich aroma. We do not blend with corn syrup, sugar syrup, or foreign honey.

Why does pure raw honey crystallize?

Crystallization is the hallmark of 100% genuine, unpasteurized honey! Real raw honey naturally solidifies over time depending on the natural glucose-to-fructose ratio and ambient temperature. Commercial syrup does not crystallize because it is heavily heated and adulterated. To liquefy raw honey, gently place the jar in a warm water bowl.

How do I place an order and make payment?

Simply choose your preferred variety and weight, then click any "Order on WhatsApp" button. A pre-filled WhatsApp message will open connecting you directly with our team at +91 91034 25071. We confirm your address and provide secure UPI / Bank transfer payment options.

How fast is delivery in Delhi NCR and across India?

For Delhi NCR (Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad, Faridabad), orders are dispatched promptly and typically arrive within 24 to 48 hours. Pan-India shipments are dispatched via express tracked courier taking 3–5 working days.
